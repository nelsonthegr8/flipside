How it plays

Ask, flip, guess.

Each player secretly picks one face from a board of 24. Take turns asking yes-or-no questions and flipping down the faces that don't fit. Name your opponent's face first to win.

01

Pick your card

Tap the face your opponent has to find, or let the game deal one.

02

Ask a question

“Wears glasses?” “Has a beard?” The answer comes straight from their card, so nobody can fib.

03

Flip and guess

Flip down the faces that don't match. Smart Assist can do it for you. Guess when you're sure.

Made for iPhone Duo

Two screens. One secret each.

  • Face to face. Stand the phone between you. Player 1 plays on the inner screen while Player 2 plays on the cover screen, at the same time.
  • Built around the fold. Boards and buttons stay clear of the crease and the camera, open flat or half-folded like a tiny laptop.
  • Every iPhone welcome. On other iPhones, pass the phone across the table between turns.
  • Play from anywhere. Over FaceTime with SharePlay, or online through Game Center, each player on their own phone.
