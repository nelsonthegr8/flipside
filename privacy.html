Privacy Policy

Flip Side: Guess the Face · Effective November 18, 2026

In short: Flip Side has no accounts and sends no personal data to us. Online games send only game moves between players, through Apple. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and ad data to serve and measure those ads. Buying the Full Game turns ads off. Purchases are handled by Apple.

What the game itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. Flip Side has no sign-in, no profiles and no servers of its own. Your settings (cast, look and rules) are saved only on your device.

The camera on iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo, Flip Side asks for camera access. iOS only lets an app show content on the cover screen while the phone is open if the camera is running, so the game runs the camera during face-to-face play to keep Player 2's screen on.

  • The camera image is not recorded, saved, analysed or sent anywhere.
  • The camera only runs while a game is open on an unfolded iPhone Duo, and stops when you fold the phone or leave the game.
  • If you say no, you can still play: the game switches to taking turns by folding the phone.

Online play

You can play with a friend on another phone over FaceTime (SharePlay) or through Game Center. Only game moves are sent between the two phones, such as which question was asked or which face was guessed, through Apple's SharePlay or Game Center services. Nothing passes through servers of ours, and we don't receive your FaceTime, Game Center or contact details. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a full-screen ad after every second finished game, never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only, and Flip Side does not ask for permission to track you across other apps.

To deliver, cap and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Flip Side shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time from Shop → Privacy choices.

Turning ads off: the Full Game purchase removes all ads. After that, no ad requests are made.

Purchases

The Full Game is an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase is owned, so it can unlock content and restore it on your other devices.

Children

Flip Side is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are non-personalized.

Data sharing and sale

We don't sell or share personal data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above, in the free version.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub. Please don't include personal information in public issues.
