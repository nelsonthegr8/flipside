Playing on iPhone Duo

How do two people play on one iPhone Duo?

Open the phone and stand it between you, or half-fold it like a tiny laptop. Player 1 uses the inner screen and Player 2 uses the cover screen, both at the same time. Each of you picks a secret face, then you take turns asking questions.

Why does Flip Side ask for the camera?

iOS only lets an app use the cover screen while the phone is open if the camera is running. Flip Side runs it only to keep Player 2's screen on. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. If you'd rather not allow it, the game still works: you take turns by folding the phone and handing it over.

Player 2's screen is blank.

Check that camera access is on in Settings → Flip Side → Camera, then start a new game with the phone open. If another app is using the camera, close it first.

Playing on other iPhones

Can I play without an iPhone Duo?

Yes. On any iPhone, pass the phone across the table between turns. A handoff screen hides each board from the other player.

Playing online

How do I play with a friend over FaceTime?

Your friend doesn't need the app. Call them on FaceTime, tap Share → Share My Screen, then in Flip Side tap Online → Start FaceTime game. Your screen splits in two: their board is on the shared side, and yours is hidden from the share. Your friend picks a face in their head. They ask you questions from their board; you ask yours out loud and tap the answer they give.

How does my friend flip faces on their board?

They can tell you which faces to flip, or tap them directly if they request remote control of your shared screen in FaceTime.

Can we play online without FaceTime?

Yes. Tap Online → Find a match to invite a friend through Game Center or Messages, or to play someone new. Both of you need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center).

Do we both need the Full Game?

No. Over FaceTime, only you need the app. Online, the host's pack and look are used, so only one of you needs the Full Game.

Asking your own questions

Can I ask something that isn't on the list?

Yes. Open the questions and use Ask your own: ask out loud, then tap the answer you heard. You can also end your turn at any point.

Purchases and ads

What does the Full Game include?

Every character pack (Masquerade, Spotlight, Capes, The Feed, Critters and Kingdom), every look, all future packs, and no ads. It's a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Flip Side, tap Shop, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How often do ads show?

In the free version, one short ad after every second finished game (after each match in Speed Duel). Never during a game, and none in your first few games.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EU, UK and Switzerland, open Shop → Privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
